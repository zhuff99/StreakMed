Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Overview

StreakMed ("the App") is a medication tracking application built to help you manage your daily medications and build healthy habits. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

Data Collection

StreakMed does not collect any personal data.

All information you enter into the App — including medication names, dosages, schedules, and tracking history — is stored exclusively on your device using Apple's built-in on-device storage (CoreData). This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any server, third party, or external service, with one optional exception you control: the Prescription Scanning feature described below, which only operates if you enable it and only when you actively use it.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any account information
  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools
  • We do not serve advertisements
  • We do not share data with third parties
  • We do not have access to any information you enter into the App

Notifications

If you enable medication reminders, the App uses Apple's local notification system to schedule alerts on your device. These notifications are generated and delivered entirely on-device. No notification content is transmitted to any external server.

Data Export

The App includes an optional Export History feature that allows you to generate a copy of your medication history as a CSV or PDF file. This export is created entirely on your device. The App does not transmit this file anywhere automatically.

When you choose to share an exported file using iOS's share sheet (for example, saving it to Files, emailing it, or sending it via a messaging app), that file leaves your device and is subject to the privacy policies of the service you share it with. You are responsible for how exported files are handled once shared.

Prescription Scanning (Optional)

The App includes an optional AI-powered prescription label scanning feature. To use this feature, you must supply your own API key from Anthropic (console.anthropic.com). This key is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device — it is never synced, never included in backups, and never shared with or transmitted to StreakMed.

When you use the scanning feature:

  • Your photo never leaves your device. The label text is read from the image entirely on-device using Apple's Vision framework.
  • Identifying details are removed before anything is sent. Phone numbers, addresses, dates, and prescription (Rx) numbers detected in the label text are stripped on your device.
  • The remaining text (medication name, strength, and directions) is sent securely to Anthropic's Claude API using your own key, solely to identify the medication and fill in the form for you. This happens only when you tap "Scan," and never automatically.
  • This transmission is governed by Anthropic's Privacy Policy (anthropic.com/legal/privacy). Anthropic does not use API data to train its models. StreakMed has no server and no access to the content of these requests or responses.

If you do not use this feature, no data is ever sent off your device.

Apple Health (Optional)

The App can optionally read your medication list from Apple Health (iOS 26 or later) to save you from typing your medications in manually. This requires your explicit permission through Apple's Health access prompt, and access can be revoked at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices. Imported medication information is stored on your device like all other App data. StreakMed only reads your medication list — it does not read any other Health data, and it does not write anything to Apple Health. Health data is never transmitted off your device by StreakMed.

Data Storage and Security

All App data is stored locally on your device and is subject to your device's built-in security features, including passcode protection and data encryption provided by iOS. If you delete the App, all associated data is permanently removed from your device.

Children's Privacy

StreakMed is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided information through this App, please contact us — though we note that the App stores no data outside of your device.
